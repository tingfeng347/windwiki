# Research

Turn paper reading, experiment reproduction and research writing into traceable knowledge.

## Scope

Papers, experiments, benchmarks and writing.

While reading, separate the authors' assumptions, conclusions supported by experiments and your own inferences. When reproducing, record the code version, data split, random seeds and runtime environment.

## Start here

Organize content by specific research question and keep links to the original papers and experimental evidence. Benchmark comparisons should use consistent data and metric definitions.
